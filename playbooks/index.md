# Playbooks

Each playbook takes you from nothing to something working, one short chapter at a time. Your AI assistant does the technical parts: you mostly tell it what you want.

Start here:

- **[Get Edgible running](get-edgible-running/):** the first chapter of every playbook.
- **[Getting a machine](getting-a-machine/):** no spare computer? A virtual machine on yours, or a small cloud server.
- **[Use your own domain](use-your-own-domain/):** give any app an address on a domain you own.

  3. [Your AI gateway](your-ai-gateway/): the cost guardrails, first
  4. [Your agent](your-agent/)

  6. [A home for everything you've built](a-home-for-your-apps/)

  1. [Add a second machine](second-machine/)
  2. [Your private model](your-private-model/)
  3. [Private in your gateway](private-in-your-gateway/)
  4. [Private for your agent](private-for-your-agent/)
  5. [Choosing between them](choosing-between-them/)
