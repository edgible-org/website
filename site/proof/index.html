The tunnel wish list, given a fair try

The awesome-tunneling list describes the tunnel its author wants. Every app gets an address automatically, with its HTTPS certificate issued for it. It carries web and TCP traffic, and is managed from a console or the command line. The agent installs under your own account, with no sudo. Where an operating system asks for more, as macOS can, Edgible tells you as you install. Your own domain needs one DNS record at your current provider, and nothing to move.
