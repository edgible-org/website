Why Edgible, why now

A billion agents.A trillion apps.*

We are entering a world where the way people and companies use computing changes for good. Software stops being something you buy and starts being something you ask for: highly customised, bespoke, targeted apps, built for "free" by an AI, for one person, one team or one afternoon.

Every one of those apps, and every agent that uses them, works only by knowing us: our files, our habits, our clients, our children. The question that decides everything is where all of that lives. Edgible is the answer that keeps it with you.

The world we're entering

The easy place to run it all is someone else's cloud.

When software costs almost nothing to make, the scarce thing is trust. Agents only work well when they know us, and today the convenient way to run them is on a big platform's cloud, where every agent's memory, every app's records and every question we ask becomes data held, and often used, by someone else.

People and companies will want the other option: the same convenience, with the data on machines they control. That option has to be safe, easy and looked after, or it will stay a hobby. That is the market we are building for.

What exists today

An open catalog that runs on Edgible itself.

We built the front door in the open. A growing library of tested, self-hosted apps (cards), the card-kit that tests and publishes them, and an AI skill that turns a person's need into a running app. All of it is free and public, and every app in it ends with the same step: publish with Edgible.
